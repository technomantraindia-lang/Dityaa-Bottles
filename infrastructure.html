Unit-1 Facility Ranoli, Vadodara

Ranoli Plant — PET & ISBM Center

F.G. Patel Estate, Ranoli, Vadodara, Gujarat - 391250

Engineered specifically for high-speed primary pharmaceutical packaging. Equipped with Japanese Nissei ASB single-stage Injection Stretch Blow Moulding (ISBM) lines, this unit produces crystal-clear and amber UV-filtered oral syrup bottles from 30ml to 500ml with micron-accurate neck threads.

ISBM Single-Stage Moulding Japanese ASB lines eliminate preform scuffs and neck seam flash.
UV Amber Shielding Spectrophotometer-verified masterbatch blocks UV light up to 450nm.
Sealed HEPA Conveyors Enclosed positive-pressure conveyor tunnels connecting to shrink packers.
60M Units/Year Output Continuous 24/7 automated production ensuring uninterrupted pharma supplies.

Unit-2 Facility Padra, Vadodara

Padra Plant — HDPE & Closure Systems

At-Po, Mujpur, Ta- Padra, Dist- Vadodara, Gujarat - 391440

Specialized center for high-barrier HDPE solid dosage packaging, pill packers, ophthalmic droppers, and child-resistant closures. Housing multi-cavity Extrusion Blow Moulding (EBM) and precision injection presses with in-line camera vision testing and automated wad insertion.

HDPE Solid Packers High moisture-barrier containers for tablets, capsules, and nutraceuticals.
CRC Safety Closures Certified 28mm, 33mm, 38mm push-and-turn child-resistant two-piece caps.
Induction Liner Wadding Automated insertion of foil, foam, and heat-induction wads for zero leakage.
Calibrated Droppers 10ml & 15ml ophthalmic dropper plugs with tamper-evident tear rings.
Unit-2 Padra: Automated EBM & CRC Moulding
