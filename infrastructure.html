Manufacturing Ecosystem

Two Dedicated Plants in Vadodara

Our dual manufacturing locations in Ranoli and Padra feature Class 100,000 cleanroom environments, automated robotic packaging lines, and dedicated tooling facilities.

Unit-1 Facility

Ranoli Plant — PET & SBM Center

Located at the prestigious F.G. Patel Estate in Ranoli, Vadodara, Unit-1 is engineered specifically for high-speed pharmaceutical PET oral liquid bottle production.

Equipped with multi-cavity Injection Stretch Blow Moulding (ISBM) machines, this plant produces crystal-clear and amber UV-filtered syrup bottles from 30ml to 500ml capacities with flawless neck threads.

ISBM Technology
Single-stage stretch blow molding for zero preform scratch marks.
UV Amber Filtration
Specialized masterbatch dosing for photo-sensitive oral formulations.
Automated Conveyors
Sealed HEPA tunnel conveyors connecting machines to shrink packers.
Capacity: 60M/Year
Continuous high-volume round-the-clock commercial manufacturing.
Unit-1 Ranoli: High-Speed Nissei ASB Lines
Unit-2 Facility

Padra Plant — HDPE & Closure Systems

Situated at At-Po, Mujpur, Ta- Padra, Dist- Vadodara, Unit-2 is our specialized center for high-barrier HDPE solid dosage bottles, pill packers, ophthalmic droppers, and child-resistant caps (CRC).

The Padra plant houses automated Extrusion Blow Moulding (EBM) and high-tonnage precision injection moulding presses with integrated camera leak testing and automated liner wadding.

HDPE Solid Packers
Moisture barrier containers for tablets, hard capsules & softgels.
CRC Safety Caps
Certified 28mm, 33mm, 38mm push-and-turn two-piece closures.
Induction Liner Wadding
Automated insertion of foil, foam, and heat-induction wads.
Precision Droppers
10ml & 15ml calibrated nozzle dropper assemblies.
Unit-2 Padra: Automated EBM & CRC Moulding
Controlled Environments

Class 100,000 (ISO 8) Cleanroom Specifications

Primary pharmaceutical containers must enter filling lines without foreign airborne contaminants, hair, dust, or grease.

ISO 8 Cleanroom Environment

Positive-Pressure Automated Packaging Enclosure

Airborne Particulate Standard < 3,520,000 particles/m³ @ 0.5µm
HEPA Filtered Air

0.3-micron terminal HEPA filtration with continuous positive pressure differentials to prevent ingress.

25+ Air Changes/Hour

Advanced Air Handling Units (AHU) maintaining controlled temperature (22°C ± 2°C) and relative humidity.

Strict Gowning Air-Locks

Double-door interlocking air-shower entries with mandatory lint-free cleanroom apparel for all personnel.

Double Poly-Liner Packing

Bottles are packed directly into virgin food-grade poly bags and corrugated cartons inside cleanroom air.
