About Our Company

Reliable Polymers for Life-Saving Products

Dityaa Bottles manufactures world-class primary pharmaceutical packaging across certified Class 100,000 cleanrooms in Vadodara, Gujarat. Dedicated to absolute polymer purity, micron precision, and zero-defect sterility.

Company Profile

Finest Manufacturers of Precision Plastic Bottles & Containers

DITYAA BOTTLES is recognized in the market to be amongst the finest manufacturers and suppliers of high-barrier Plastic Bottles, PET Bottles & Jars, HDPE Packers, and Tamper-Evident Caps. Our manufactured range is praised across the pharmaceutical and healthcare sectors for superior dimensional finishing, wall uniformity, durability, and leak resistance.

Headquartered at the Broadway Commercial Hub in Bhayli, Vadodara, every container is moulded in strict compliance with current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) utilizing only 100% prime virgin resins and modern machinery. Our futuristic manufacturing operations are distributed across specialized plants equipped with single-stage Japanese ISBM lines, automated extrusion blow moulding, and 24/7 HEPA-filtered cleanrooms.

Regular technological upgrading ensures our clients enjoy the highest quality standards, zero particulate contamination, competitive pricing, and client-centric collaboration — helping us partner with leading multinational pharmaceutical and nutraceutical corporations.

01

Dual Specialized Facilities

Ranoli & Padra Plants

Operating two dedicated manufacturing plants across Vadodara — Unit-1 (Ranoli) for single-stage Japanese PET ISBM oral liquids and Unit-2 (Padra) for automated HDPE packers and closures, guaranteeing multi-site supply redundancy.

02

Certified Class 100,000 Cleanrooms

HEPA Filtered Positive Air

All moulding, trimming, and bagging take place within certified Class 100,000 cleanrooms with continuous positive pressure air circulation and automated pneumatic resin feeding to eliminate airborne particulates and cross-contamination.

03

Japanese Nissei ASB Lines

Single-Stage Precision ISBM

Single-stage Injection Stretch Blow Moulding lines produce preform and container in one continuous thermal cycle, guaranteeing uniform wall thickness distribution, crystal optical transparency, and flawless 28mm neck calibration.

04

USP <671> UV & Moisture Barrier

Spectral Light Protection

Certified spectral light transmission protection (<10% transmission up to 450nm) shielding photosensitive syrups and tonics, plus high moisture vapor barrier HDPE for solid oral dosage tablets and capsules.

05

Hermetic Closures & Dosing Cups

28mm ROPP, CRC & Cups

Precision-matched child-resistant caps (CRC), tamper-evident pilfer-proof closures, and calibrated dosing cups (2.5ml - 10ml) engineered for flawless sealing without torque jamming on high-speed automated packaging lines.

06

Complete Audit Dossiers & COA

Full Regulatory Traceability

Every delivery includes lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA), migration test certificates, US FDA 21 CFR compliance declarations, and full technical documentation for domestic and international regulatory audits.
