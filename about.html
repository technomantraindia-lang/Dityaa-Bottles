About Our Company

Reliable Polymers for Life-Saving Products

Dityaa Bottles was founded with a singular conviction: primary packaging for pharmaceutical formulations must uphold the exact same purity, precision, and sterility as the life-saving medicine it protects.

Company Profile

Finest Manufacturers of Precision Plastic Bottles & Containers

DITYAA BOTTLES is considered in the market to be amongst the finest manufacturers and suppliers of this highly commendable range of Plastic Bottles, Pet Plastic Jars, Pet Bottles, and Plastic Bottle Caps. Our offered range is widely praised for its superior finishing, sturdiness, durability, and resistance to leakage.

Headquartered at the Broadway Commercial Hub in Bhayli, Vadodara, making of this range is done as per the norms and guidelines of the industry utilizing the finest virgin raw materials and modern machinery. The highly futuristic state-of-the-art infrastructural facility at DITYAA BOTTLES has been parted into several specialized units equipped with modern machinery to manage operations in the most efficient and effective manner.

Our facility, regularly upgraded, helps us in attaining a leading position in the packaging industry. In addition to this, our ethical work practices, competitive pricing, and client-centric behavior have helped us acquire and retain several highly reputed multinational pharmaceutical and FMCG clients.
