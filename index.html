About Dityaa Bottles

Engineered for Purity, Certified for Patient Safety

Headquartered at the Broadway Commercial Hub in Bhayli, Vadodara, DITYAA BOTTLES is considered in the market to be amongst the finest manufacturers and suppliers of plastic bottles, pet jars, pet bottles, and bottle caps — widely praised for superior finishing, sturdiness, durability, and leak resistance.

Operating two advanced production units across Ranoli (Unit-1) and Padra (Unit-2), making of this range is done as per industry guidelines utilizing prime virgin raw materials and modern machinery. We specialize in primary pharmaceutical packaging, syrup bottles, pill containers, dropper assemblies, and wide-mouth containers.

Certified Class 100,000 Cleanroom Moulding

Positive-pressure cleanrooms with continuous HEPA air handling eliminate particulate bio-burden during injection stretch & extrusion blow moulding.

100% Virgin Resins with Traceable CoA

Sourced exclusively from Reliance and IOCL with full Certificate of Analysis (CoA) traceability, ensuring zero extractables and zero heavy metals.

Dual-Plant Supply Resilience (Ranoli & Padra)

Strategic twin-facility manufacturing guarantees seamless operational redundancy, eliminating single-source supply chain vulnerabilities.

Zero-Defect Quality Control

In-House Analytical & Physical Testing Lab

Every production batch undergoes strict pharmacopoeial inspection to ensure seamless leak-proof capping, uniform wall thickness, and drop resilience.

Vacuum Leakage Testing (-550 mmHg)

Ensures airtight seals between cap, induction liner, and neck thread under negative air pressure chamber.

Optical Wall Thickness & Weight Profiling

Multi-point ultrasonic thickness gauges prevent weak thin-spots, ensuring uniform rigidity across corners.

Torque & Child-Resistance Activation

Digital torque meters verify removal and application torque limits for effortless dispensing without cap spin-offs.

Drop Impact & Environmental Stress Cracking

1.2-meter filled drop tests verify high impact tolerance during pharmaceutical transit and distribution.
